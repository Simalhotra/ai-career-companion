Resume Review Checklist for Resume Clinic
GENERAL 
* Ensure correct spelling and grammar
* Avoid the use of first and third person pronouns, such as "I," "he," or "she."
* Keep verb tenses consistent throughout. 
* Use present tense for current activities and employment
* Use past tense for activities and employment not presently held
* Use action verbs and concise statements to articulate relevant experiences
* Avoid using each verb or adjective more than once
* Remove images, columns, tables, fields, text boxes, and graphics so application tracking systems can quickly scan your text for keywords and phrase
* Spell out all acronyms the first time they are used, followed by the acronym in parentheses
* Be consistent with alignment and appropriately use left, center, and right alignment
* Make use of bold, underline, and italics to emphasize different aspects of your resume, such as headings and titles
* The resume makes mention of transferable skills: Career & Self Development, Communication, Critical Thinking, Equity & Inclusion, Leadership, Professionalism, Teamwork, Technology
* The document should look balanced and pleasing to the eyes, and easy to read
* The resume should be organized in reverse chronological order, meaning the most recent experiences should be listed first, following your previous experiences 
* Use a variety of action verbs to bring skills and experiences to life, refer handout
* Do not use "responsible for" to describe experiences 
* Emphasize results/outcome/quantifiable numbers
* Resume must be on one page and 2 pages for Graduate students, if need be
* Check for indentation and consistency in font size
HEADER
* Applicant's name should be the largest text on the page and is line 1 of the resume
* Try and have a LinkedIn Link or Github URL on line 2 of resume
* City, State is sufficient. Include LinkedIn URL and keep contact details on one line
OBJECTIVE/SUMMARY
* Objectives/summaries are optional. If need be include it, typically two sentences long
* The most effective objective is one that is tailored to the job you are applying for
EDUCATION
* Standard formatting recommends?that education should be at the top, followed by experience, but this can vary
* Ensure institution names are in full
* Include graduation date or expected graduation date
* Optional: Honors, GPA, Major GPA, Relevant Coursework
WORK EXPERIENCE
* Keep the most relevant job experience at the top or the most recent one
* Always include employer, job title, location, and duration of employment
* Avoid making the mistake of simply listing tasks
* Focus on demonstrating that you helped the company solve its problems and achieve its goals 
* Include internships, academic/personal projects, volunteer experience in your experience section to list a few

SKILLS
* Match your skills closely to?the job requirements while including skills that will compliment those that are being sought
* Try and explain proficiency level and fluency with language skills
* Differentiate between technical and language skills
* Do not just list soft skills here. Instead use skills in describing work experiences
